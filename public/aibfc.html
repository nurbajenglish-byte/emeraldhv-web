Questions

Everything you need before you register.

Who can enter?

Any student in Grades 7–12, from any school, in any country. Financial Literacy is for Grades 7–9; Entrepreneurship for Grades 10–12; the optional full-service package is open to all grades.

Do students need a finance background?

No. This is the first edition and it’s built to be beginner-friendly. Every registration includes free practice exams, study sets and games so a motivated first-timer can prepare from scratch.

What does the finals exam look like?

Both tracks take an offline exam at Hong Kong Science Park on Saturday 5 December: registration at 10:00, exam at 11:00. It is a 40-question multiple-choice exam, 60 minutes, taken offline. It is similar to our practice exams, with some more topics added. Download the finals prep guide (PDF) covering both tracks.

What does the full-service package include?

Meet and see-off at the airport, accommodation in a twin room at a 3-star hotel, all meals, and a city sightseeing programme with a choice of Disneyland or a top university visit (CUHK) with the group. It also includes the museum, tram, cable car and a cruise dinner. The package is $799 per student: book it here, download the full package PDF, or contact us for details.

Do we have to travel?

Yes — the finals exam is offline, at Hong Kong Science Park on 5 December, with the award ceremony on 6 December. Registration is open until 26 November. Flights are arranged by each family; an optional $799 full-service package is available — see what is included.

Are there team events?

No. Both tracks, including Entrepreneurship, are individual. Every student takes the finals exam on 5 December and receives their own result.

What does every participant get?

An official certificate, the award ceremony T-shirt, a medal at the ceremony and a small remembrance gift, all included in the $100 registration. Medals are Gold, Silver or Bronze.

How and when do we pay?

Registration is paid securely online by card. Registration is $100 per student, all tracks.

Can one teacher register several students?

Yes. A school coordinator can register multiple students in bulk and manage them from one account. Email us to be set up as a coordinator.

What is the refund policy?

Full refunds are available until 20 November 2026. After that date, registration fees are non-refundable, as finals logistics are committed. To request a refund, email emeraldhv@nurbakyt.com. If A-IBFC 2026 is cancelled by the organisers, all fees are refunded in full.
